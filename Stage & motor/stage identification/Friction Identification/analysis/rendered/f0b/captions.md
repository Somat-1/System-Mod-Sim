# F0b results figures — captions

**F0b-1. A reversal costs 2.30 µm, and takes about 8 µm of travel to pay off — two and a half times the pre-sliding length F1 and F2 were planned on.** (a) Lost motion against stroke from the reversal ladder at $x_0$ = 60 mm, as commanded stroke minus measured travel between the settled window means at the two turning points. Filled: increasing pass; open: decreasing pass, dodged right; grey diamonds: the first half-cycle of each stroke, which starts from whatever the previous stroke left rather than from a closed loop. Error bars are ±2σ over half-cycles. Small strokes lose almost all their motion (dashed diagonal); the loss saturates at 2.30 µm, about 30 % above $\beta_0$ = 1.757 µm from E2–E5. The two passes agree to 126 nm, so the ladder carries no history beyond the stroke itself and F1 needs no extra wipes between strokes. Strokes above 100 µm are not shown: their rungs run at over 25 µm/s and do not settle within the 0.5 s turning-point pause. (b) The same data as the fraction of the commanded stroke that reaches the carriage; the light line is a reading aid. (c) The same quantity measured continuously instead, from the slow ramps at 0.25 µm/s after a 3 mm wipe (heavy) and continuing in the same direction (light); their difference is the part that a reversal adds, and it is taken over the same commanded positions so the geometric and microstep error cancel. The carriage leaves the stuck line after 40 nm, one microstep, and has still not saturated after 20 µm. The ladder points fall within the band the two ramp directions span over the whole 1–20 µm range, two independent measurements of the same quantity from different blocks of the session; the difference between the up and down ramps, 0.3–0.5 µm, is as large as any difference between the methods, and is itself the direction asymmetry F1 checks at three positions.

**F0b-2. The session resolves 2.1 nm, 19 times finer than a microstep, and moves two of the three numbers F1 and F2 were planned on.** (a) Precision of a window mean against averaging time at the middle and both ends of the travel, as the overlapping Allan deviation after removing a linear trend from each hold; the dashed line is the white-noise slope. At the 0.5 s record window (open markers) the precision is 0.50 nm, and the three positions agree within 0.10 nm, so the floor does not depend on position. (b) The first 30 microsteps of a slow ramp: the commanded staircase advances 39 nm every 156 ms and the carriage follows it smoothly, at a mean 20 nm per step after a reversal. No step delivers less than $d_{\min}$ and none delivers more than twice the mean, so there is no stick-slip: the carriage simply arrives short, and single 1/256 steps are usable. (c) Lost motion after a reversal following 1 s and 60 s at rest, two trials each in ABBA order; the band is ±$d_{\min}$ about the mean of the short-dwell trials. The difference is -40 nm against a trial-to-trial scatter of 66 nm, so although it exceeds the detection limit it is not separable from the scatter on two repetitions: the probe is underpowered rather than null, and F2 is not decided by it. Inset: creep during the 60 s rest. (d) Each F0b-dependent number against what F1 and F2 were planned on; open circle planned, filled measured. The speed probe is not drawn: reading a lag part-way through a move needs the commanded position at that instant, and the segment log gives only start and end times with up to 26 ms of overhead, worth tens of microns at 1 mm/s. At 0.01 mm/s, where that is sub-micron, the sliding lag is 0.70 µm against $\beta_0/2$ = 0.88 µm.

**F0b-A1. The session is valid: 346 of 346 segments, no lost steps, no driver faults.** (a) Drift of the reference-pair mean at 15 mm (filled, solid) and 105 mm (open, dashed), each referred to its own first visit; averaging the up and down approaches removes the reversal error and leaves the slow change. The two ends move differently, so the drift is a change in the stage rather than in the instrument, and the correction is interpolated in position as well as in time. (b) Standard deviation of each 0.5 s record window; all are below the threshold once the 50 ms trailing guard is applied, without which three of the twelve reference windows reach 2.1–2.9 µm because the two free-running clocks have slipped far enough by the end of the session for the next move to start inside the window. (c) MSCNT at every return to $x_0$: one value throughout. (d) Checks.
