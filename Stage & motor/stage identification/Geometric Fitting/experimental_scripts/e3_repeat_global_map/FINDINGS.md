## The two things that govern everything below

**1. Same targets, not new ones.** Same seed 5315, identical 400 targets, different day. The 400 # TARGET lines of the two logs are identical; e2_config.json seed_start was not changed. The consequence is that **Q1 as written is not answerable from this session**: the map is tested only where it was fitted. What the design gives instead is that residuals pair target by target, so the between-day comparison needs no smoothing and no interpolation.

**2. The reference moved -5.41 µm during the session**, against a ±0.5 µm criterion, at -18 µm/h over the second half. It is not noise (visits within a group agree to 0.17 µm) and not a lost step (MSCNT identical at all seven references). The mirrored pass design holds each target's mean visit time constant to 0.013 s, so drift that is linear in time cancels from ȳ — D0 and D1 return ȳ shapes agreeing to 2.2e-05 µm — while B, which differences the directions, takes the full excursion over an up-to-down gap running 68–2225 s.

Which results that leaves standing:

| Quantity | Spread over D0/D1/D2 | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| A₁ (mean over x) | 2.3e-04 µm | unaffected |

| gain | 24.5 ppm | **D2 only** |

The spread is **not an error bar**: it is contamination removed by D2, not an uncertainty. D0 applies no correction and D1, given the mirrored design, provably removes only a constant from ȳ, so with a drift this large neither is a candidate answer. What is genuinely unbounded is the adequacy of a piecewise-linear drift model through three references.

## What to do next

1. **Change `seed_start` in `e2_config.json` and re-run.** This is the one-line change that turns a replication into the new-position test the procedure was written for, and it is the only open question that the stage itself can answer.
2. **Find the 5.4 µm drift.** It appeared entirely in the second half at −18 µm/h, and no temperature was logged, so it cannot even be tested against the 11.5 ppm/K steel coefficient. Log air and stage temperature before the next session.
3. **Add reference visits.** Three anchors cannot distinguish a real s(x) change from drift curvature within a segment, and that ambiguity is now the limit on the 1.76 µm s(x) difference. Visiting the reference every tenth stop would make the D3 variant the procedure anticipates available, and would turn this session's biggest unknown into a measurement.
