# K and M Matrices Used in the MAC / Co-MAC Derivations
Both Co-MAC methods in this folder ([Split Method](Split%20Method/comac_mode_extraction.md), [Kinematically Scaled](Kinematically%20Scaled/comac_kinematic_scaled.md)) start from the identical pair of generalized eigenproblems `scipy.linalg.eigh(K, M)`:

- **Set A** (frictionless baseline) -- `M0, C0, K0, Bu0 = build_bode_rev4.build_matrices(p)`
- **Set B** (frozen-linearized LuGre, `V_STAGE = 5.0 mm/s`) -- `M1, K1, C1, Bem1 = run_local_linearization_bode.build_linearized_matrices(p)`

Every MAC and COMAC number in either report is a downstream function of these two $(K, M)$ pairs. This page renders them once, symbolically and numerically, instead of re-deriving them inside each sub-method's report.

## Does this carry the corrected/final model form?
**Yes.** Both builder functions implement [`state_space_6dof.md`](../../../../state_space_6dof.md) Sec. 5.2-5.4 as it stands after the 2026-08-18 fix, not an earlier draft:

- **Sec. 10, items 1-3 (resolved this revision):** the screw-nut interface is embedded directly in $\mathbf{K}$/$\mathbf{C}$ through the lead ratio $L/2\pi$ rather than injected as a separate frozen-friction input, so the reaction pair on $\theta_s$ and $x_s$ is symmetric by construction and there is exactly one screw-nut element (no double path).
- **Sec. 5.3 sign correction (2026-08-18):** the four off-diagonal $\tfrac{L}{2\pi}k_{nut}$ terms have the sign fixed so that positive $\theta_s$ drives positive $x_n$, per the Sec. 1 convention. An earlier draft had the opposite sign there, which inverted the command-to-stage DC gain (a spurious 180 deg offset at low frequency). `build_bode_rev4.py`'s own docstring records this explicitly (*"2026-08-18 sign correction"*), and the numeric $K_0$ below is generated by that exact function -- not hand-transcribed.
- **Sec. 5.4 ($c_{EM}$ in $\mathbf{C}$):** `C[0,0] = c_c + c_EM` is applied by default in both builders, matching the 2026-08-18 default-inclusion decision (mode 1 is otherwise almost undamped). This does not affect the $K$/$M$ shown below, but it is part of the same fixed derivation and is included in both builder functions for that reason.

$\mathbf{C}$ itself is **not** used anywhere in the MAC/Co-MAC computation -- `eigh(K, M)` is the undamped generalized eigenproblem. $\mathbf{C}$ only matters for the time-domain and Bode simulations elsewhere in this project, not for this modal comparison. It is mentioned here only to confirm both builders are the single, consistent, corrected implementation of Sec. 5, not two independently-patched copies.

## Set A -- Frictionless Baseline, Stiffness Matrix $\mathbf{K}_0$
Symbolic form (Sec. 5.3, corrected sign):

$$\mathbf{K} = \begin{bmatrix}k_c+k_{EM}+k_d & -k_c & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\-k_c & k_c+k_{s1} & -k_{s1} & 0 & 0 & 0 \\0 & -k_{s1} & k_{s1}+k_{s2}+\left(\tfrac{L}{2\pi}\right)^2 k_{nut} & -k_{s2} & \tfrac{L}{2\pi}k_{nut} & -\tfrac{L}{2\pi}k_{nut} \\0 & 0 & -k_{s2} & k_{s2} & 0 & 0 \\0 & 0 & \tfrac{L}{2\pi}k_{nut} & 0 & k_{brg}+k_{nut} & -k_{nut} \\0 & 0 & -\tfrac{L}{2\pi}k_{nut} & 0 & -k_{nut} & k_{nut}\end{bmatrix}$$

with $L/2\pi = 1.591549e-04$ m/rad, and the nut treated as fully stuck ($k_{nut} = 1.000e+08$ N/m, no LuGre softening).

## Set B -- Frozen-Linearized LuGre System, Stiffness Matrix $\mathbf{K}_1$
Same base structure as $\mathbf{K}_0$, with the nut's rigid $k_{nut}$ replaced by its frozen LuGre equivalent stiffness $K_{eq,nut}$ (bristle deflection frozen at the steady-state operating point, Jacobian taken numerically), and two new grounding terms added on the diagonal for the other two friction ports -- $K_{eq,sb}$ on $\theta_{sb}$ and $K_{eq,way}$ on $x_n$ -- which do not appear in $\mathbf{K}_0$ at all (those ports are frictionless inputs there, held at zero, not stiffness terms):

$$\mathbf{K} = \begin{bmatrix}k_c+k_{EM}+k_d & -k_c & 0 & 0 & 0 & 0 \\-k_c & k_c+k_{s1} & -k_{s1} & 0 & 0 & 0 \\0 & -k_{s1} & k_{s1}+k_{s2}+\left(\tfrac{L}{2\pi}\right)^2 K_{eq,nut} & -k_{s2} & \tfrac{L}{2\pi}K_{eq,nut} & -\tfrac{L}{2\pi}K_{eq,nut} \\0 & 0 & -k_{s2} & k_{s2}+K_{eq,sb} & 0 & 0 \\0 & 0 & \tfrac{L}{2\pi}K_{eq,nut} & 0 & k_{brg}+K_{eq,nut} & -K_{eq,nut} \\0 & 0 & -\tfrac{L}{2\pi}K_{eq,nut} & 0 & -K_{eq,nut} & K_{eq,nut}+K_{eq,way}\end{bmatrix}$$

Frozen operating point: $V_{stage} = 5.0$ mm/s, giving $\omega_0 = 31.4159$ rad/s at the screw/bearing and the following equivalent stiffnesses (numerical Jacobian of the LuGre bristle force at that frozen point):

| Port | $v_0$ | $K_{eq}$ |
|---|---|---|
| nut | 0.000e+00 m/s | 2.0000e+06 N/m |
| support bearing | 31.4159 rad/s | 5.0000e+02 N m/rad |
| guideway | 5.000e-03 m/s | 7.6000e+05 N/m |

## $\mathbf{K}_1 - \mathbf{K}_0$ -- Where Set A and Set B Actually Differ
Every MAC/COMAC number in this folder ultimately traces back to the entries that change here; everywhere else, $\mathbf{K}_0$ and $\mathbf{K}_1$ are identical:
| | theta_m | theta_c | theta_s | theta_sb | x_s | x_n |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **theta_m** | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 |
| **theta_c** | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 |
| **theta_s** | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | -2.4824e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | -1.5597e+04 | 1.5597e+04 |
| **theta_sb** | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | 5.0000e+02 | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 |
| **x_s** | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | -1.5597e+04 | 0.0000e+00 | -9.8000e+07 | 9.8000e+07 |
| **x_n** | 0.0000e+00 | 0.0000e+00 | 1.5597e+04 | 0.0000e+00 | 9.8000e+07 | -9.7240e+07 |

The nonzero block is exactly the $(\theta_s, \theta_{sb}, x_s, x_n)$ sub-structure touched by the three friction ports -- $k_{nut} \to K_{eq,nut}$ (nut, $\approx 1\times10^{8} \to \approx 2\times10^6$ N/m, a much softer effective contact once the bristle is allowed to deflect instead of assumed rigid), plus the two new grounding terms $K_{eq,sb}$ on $\theta_{sb}$ and $K_{eq,way}$ on $x_n$ that have no counterpart in $\mathbf{K}_0$ at all. $\theta_m$, $\theta_c$, and every entry not touching those DOFs are exactly zero in this difference -- the motor/coupling end of the drivetrain is untouched by the friction linearization.
