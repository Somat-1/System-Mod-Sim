1. Retain the frictionless structural backbone

The retained plant is \(\mathbf M\ddot{\mathbf q}+\mathbf C\dot{\mathbf q}+\mathbf K\mathbf q=\mathbf b\theta_{cmd}\), with

\[\mathbf M=\operatorname{diag}(I_m,I_c,I_s,I_{sb},M_{screw},M_s),\quad \mathbf b=\begin{bmatrix}k_{EM}&0&0&0&0&0\end{bmatrix}^{\mathsf T}.\]
Full retained stiffness and damping matrices
\[\mathbf K=\begin{bmatrix}k_c+k_{EM}&-k_c&0&0&0&0\\-k_c&k_c+k_{s1}&-k_{s1}&0&0&0\\0&-k_{s1}&k_{s1}+k_{s2}+\ell^2k_{nut}&-k_{s2}&\ell k_{nut}&-\ell k_{nut}\\ 0&0&-k_{s2}&k_{s2}&0&0\\0&0&\ell k_{nut}&0&k_{brg}+k_{nut}&-k_{nut}\\0&0&-\ell k_{nut}&0&-k_{nut}&k_{nut}\end{bmatrix}\]
\[\mathbf C=\begin{bmatrix}c_c+c_{EM}&-c_c&0&0&0&0\\-c_c&c_c+c_{s1}&-c_{s1}&0&0&0\\0&-c_{s1}&c_{s1}+c_{s2}+\ell^2c_{nut}&-c_{s2}&\ell c_{nut}&-\ell c_{nut}\\ 0&0&-c_{s2}&c_{s2}&0&0\\0&0&\ell c_{nut}&0&c_{brg}+c_{nut}&-c_{nut}\\0&0&-\ell c_{nut}&0&-c_{nut}&c_{nut}\end{bmatrix}.\]
Purpose. Keeping \(k_{nut}\) and \(c_{nut}\) preserves the stiff rolling-contact path that transmits thrust. Nut LuGre adds pre-rolling drag; it does not replace or cap that contact force.

2. Define the three kinematic ports

A row Jacobian measures scalar port velocity, \(v_p=\mathbf J_p\dot{\mathbf q}\). Its transpose returns the reaction: \(\mathbf Q_p=-\mathbf J_p^{\mathsf T}F_p\).

Guideway · grounded

\[\mathbf J_{way}=\begin{bmatrix}0&0&0&0&0&1\end{bmatrix},\quad v_{way}=\dot x_n\]

Stage velocity relative to ground.

Nut · internal

\[\mathbf J_{nut}=\begin{bmatrix}0&0&\ell&0&1&-1\end{bmatrix}\]\[v_{nut}=\ell\dot\theta_s+\dot x_s-\dot x_n=-\dot\delta_n\]

Slip across the retained nut contact.

Support bearing · grounded

\[\mathbf J_{sb}=\begin{bmatrix}0&0&0&1&0&0\end{bmatrix},\quad v_{sb}=\dot\theta_{sb}\]

Bearing-ring rotation relative to ground.

Purpose. One \(\mathbf J_p\) definition fixes both the observed relative velocity and all reaction signs. The internal nut port automatically produces its force pair and lead-reflected screw torque.

3. Attach one smoothed LuGre law per port

For \(p\in\{way,nut,sb\}\), define

\[g_p(v)=F_{c,p}+(F_{s,p}-F_{c,p})e^{-(v/v_{s,p})^2},\quad s_p=\sqrt{v_p^2+\varepsilon^2},\quad \lambda_p=\frac{\sigma_{0,p}s_p}{g_p(v_p)}.\]
\[\dot z_p=v_p-\lambda_pz_p,\quad F_p=\sigma_{0,p}z_p+\sigma_{1,p}\dot z_p+\sigma_{2,p}v_p.\]

Bristle state

\(z_p\) stores average deflection: presliding stiffness and reversal memory at small travel, then decay toward sliding.

Smooth speed

\(\sqrt{v_p^2+\varepsilon^2}\) removes the derivative kink at zero speed so the stiff solver can use an analytical Jacobian.

4. Assemble the 15-state nonlinear model

Load path retained

\(F_{struct}=k_{nut}\delta_n+c_{nut}\dot\delta_n\), already in \(\mathbf K,\mathbf C\).

+

Pre-rolling drag added

\(F_{nut}=\sigma_{0,n}z_n+\sigma_{1,n}\dot z_n+\sigma_{2,n}v_n\), injected through \(-\mathbf J_{nut}^{\mathsf T}F_{nut}\).

\[\dot z_p=\mathbf J_p\mathbf v-\frac{\sigma_{0,p}\sqrt{(\mathbf J_p\mathbf v)^2+\varepsilon^2}}{g_p(\mathbf J_p\mathbf v)}z_p,\qquad p\in\{way,nut,sb\}.\]
Result. Friction is a nonlinear feedback layer around the retained structure. Setting \(F_p=0\) recovers that backbone; the three bristle states add reversal memory, Stribeck behavior and velocity-dependent drag.
\[\mathbf q=[\theta_m,\theta_c,\theta_s,\theta_{sb},x_s,x_n]^{\mathsf T},\quad \mathbf x=[\mathbf q^{\mathsf T},\mathbf v^{\mathsf T},z_{way},z_{nut},z_{sb}]^{\mathsf T}.\]
\[Q_f=[0,0,-\ell F_{nut},-T_{sb},-F_{nut},F_{nut}-F_{way}]^{\mathsf T}.\]
\[M\dot v=b\theta_{cmd}-Cv-Kq-e_mT_d\sin(4N_r\theta_m)+Q_f.\]
\[\ell=L/(2\pi),\quad k_{EM}=N_rT_{hold},\quad \delta_n=x_n-x_s-\ell\theta_s.\]

5. Differentiate the same ports for the solver and Bode model

At each operating point, first differentiate the scalar port law:

\[g'_p=-\frac{2v_p}{v_{s,p}^2}(F_{s,p}-F_{c,p})e^{-(v_p/v_{s,p})^2},\quad s'_p=\frac{v_p}{s_p},\quad \lambda'_p=\sigma_{0,p}\left(\frac{s'_p}{g_p}-\frac{s_pg'_p}{g_p^2}\right).\]
\[a_p=\frac{\partial\dot z_p}{\partial v_p}=1-\lambda'_pz_p,\quad b_p=\frac{\partial\dot z_p}{\partial z_p}=-\lambda_p,\] \[c_p=\frac{\partial F_p}{\partial v_p}=\sigma_{1,p}a_p+\sigma_{2,p},\quad d_p=\frac{\partial F_p}{\partial z_p}=\sigma_{0,p}+\sigma_{1,p}b_p.\]
\[A_{vz_p}=-M^{-1}J_p^{\mathsf T}d_p,\quad A_{z_pv}=a_pJ_p,\quad A_{z_pz_p}=b_p,\quad B_v=M^{-1}b.\]
Purpose. Each scalar derivative becomes a rank-one mechanical update \(c_pJ_p^{\mathsf T}J_p\). This analytical Jacobian accelerates Radau integration and supplies the frozen local Bode model.
\[D_d=4N_rT_d\cos(4N_r\theta_m)e_me_m^{\mathsf T},\quad A_{vq}=-M^{-1}(K+D_d).\]
\[A_{vv}=-M^{-1}\left(C+\sum_pc_pJ_p^{\mathsf T}J_p\right).\]

6. Exact base LuGre comparison

The comparison replaces only the speed magnitude in the decay law:

\[\dot z_p=v_p-\sigma_{0,p}|v_p|z_p/g_p(v_p).\]
Physics retained at rest. At \(v_p=0\), exact LuGre gives \(\dot z_p=0\): stored bristle deflection is held. The smoothed law instead gives \(\dot z_p=-(\sigma_{0,p}\varepsilon/F_{s,p})z_p\).

\[\lambda'_p=\sigma_{0,p}\left(\frac{\operatorname{sign}(v_p)}{g_p}-\frac{|v_p|g'_p}{g_p^2}\right).\]

Valid zero-speed tangent

At \((v_p,z_p)=(0,0)\): \(a_p=1, b_p=0, c_p=\sigma_{1,p}+\sigma_{2,p}, d_p=\sigma_{0,p}\).

True nonsmooth point

At \(v_p=0,z_p\ne0\), no classical Jacobian exists. The implementation keeps the exact nonlinear RHS and rejects that analytical tangent.

The exact nut-memory tangent has one neutral mode. Its plotted zero-frequency point is therefore evaluated at \(10^{-6}\) Hz; every positive-frequency sample uses the exact tangent directly.

Interpretation. The local Bode result is insensitive because \(\varepsilon=10^{-9}\) is far below the active port velocities. The material difference appears in nonlinear zero-speed dwell and reversal memory, not this cruise-point small-signal response.
\[F_p=\sigma_{0,p}z_p+\sigma_{1,p}\dot z_p+\sigma_{2,p}v_p.\]

Resolved difference: exact versus smoothed is below 5.8×10⁻⁷ dB and 3.9×10⁻⁶° over 0–8 kHz at this operating point.

Nonlinear stepping comparison

Resolved stepping difference: the maximum exact-versus-smoothed deviation is 2.63 pm, occurring in the 4 ms full-step case. The 250 ms cases remain below 1.24 pm.

Parallel means additiveThe structural nut spring-damper remains; LuGre adds drag across the same relative motion.
Jacobians own the signs\(v_p=J_p\dot q\) and \(Q_p=-J_p^TF_p\) give consistent grounded and internal reactions.
Three states add the physicsEach bristle state contributes presliding stiffness, reversal memory and sliding transition.
Smoothing enables derivatives\(\sqrt{v^2+\varepsilon^2}\) makes the zero-speed decay differentiable.
